Privacy Policy

Learn how privacy is handled when you use Chronelle's clock faces, personalization tools, home screen widgets, live wallpapers, and advertising-supported features.

Effective date: October 18, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes privacy practices for Chronelle – Classic Clock (the “App”), published by Mohamed Darkaoui ("we," "us," or "our"). By using the App, you acknowledge the practices described in this policy. If you have questions, contact us at learnovaapps@gmail.com.

2. Information and app functionality

Chronelle lets you browse and customize clock designs, use clock widgets, and apply clock-themed live wallpapers. The App may process the settings and design choices needed to provide these functions, such as selected clock styles, appearance preferences, and saved customizations.

These preferences are intended to support the App's features on your device. The App does not require you to create an account to use its core clock features.

3. Advertising and third-party services

Chronelle uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to display advertising, including banner, app open, interstitial, and optional rewarded ads. Google and its advertising partners may collect or process information such as device identifiers (including advertising identifiers where available), IP address, approximate location inferred from network information, ad interactions, diagnostics, and other data according to their own policies and applicable consent choices.

Rewarded advertisements are optional and may be offered in exchange for access to certain animated clock actions. Choosing to watch a rewarded ad may involve additional advertising-related data processing by Google or its partners.

For information about Google's data practices, see Google Privacy Policy and Google AdMob policies and privacy guidance.

4. Consent and privacy choices

Where required, the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request advertising consent or present privacy choices. Depending on your region and the available consent options, you may be able to choose how advertising-related data is used.

You can also manage advertising preferences and reset or delete your advertising ID through your Android device settings, where supported. For privacy-related requests or questions, contact us at learnovaapps@gmail.com.

5. Permissions and device features

The App may use Android features and permissions necessary to display clock widgets, present live wallpapers, maintain your selected appearance, access network services for advertising, or support other requested functionality. The availability of particular permissions and system controls depends on your Android version and device.

Clock widgets and live wallpapers operate through Android's corresponding system features. The App does not need access to your contacts or personal messages to provide its core clock functionality.

6. How information is used

  • To display clocks, widgets, live wallpapers, and saved appearance settings.
  • To deliver, measure, and manage advertising and optional rewarded experiences.
  • To maintain performance, troubleshoot technical problems, and protect the App against misuse.
  • To meet applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

7. Data sharing

Advertising-related information may be processed by Google and its advertising partners to deliver and measure ads, subject to their policies and applicable consent requirements. We do not sell your personally identifiable information directly to data brokers. Third-party advertising practices are governed by the respective providers' privacy terms.

8. Data retention and deletion

App preferences and saved clock customizations may remain on your device until you change or delete them, clear the App's storage, or uninstall the App. Data processed by Google or other third-party advertising services is retained under those providers' policies. If you have a privacy or deletion request, email learnovaapps@gmail.com; we will respond as applicable to data under our control.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps to design the App with privacy and security in mind. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Third-party providers are responsible for their own systems and security practices.

10. Children's privacy

Chronelle is designed as a general-purpose clock personalization app and is not specifically directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can review the concern.

11. International processing

Third-party services such as Google AdMob may process data in countries other than your country of residence. Such processing is subject to their applicable safeguards, privacy policies, and legal obligations.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the App's functionality, services, or legal requirements change. Any revised version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Please review this page periodically.
